Material Memory This garment is translated from a simulated textile surface into a web-based 3D object, allowing Yunjin to be encountered as both fabric and apparition.

The loom becomes a portal.

This project reimagines Yunjin not as a static historical object, but as a living digital material. The garment acts as a threshold: a soft artifact suspended between craft, simulation and memory.

Instead of presenting textile heritage as a flat image, the work asks how brocade can be encountered spatially — through light, movement, digital cloth and a changing point of view.
