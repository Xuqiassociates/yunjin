Yunjin
Reverie A Fleeting Illusion

A speculative digital museum of Nanjing Yunjin brocade, where textile memory, 3D garment simulation and material reconstruction drift between cultural archive and imagined space.

Enter the loom CLO3D · Blender · Substance · Web archive
Material Memory This garment is translated from a simulated textile surface into a web-based 3D object, allowing Yunjin to be encountered as both fabric and apparition.

The loom becomes a portal.

This project reimagines Yunjin not as a static historical object, but as a living digital material. Through CLO3D, Blender and Substance-based texture development, traditional brocade aesthetics are transformed into an interactive spatial experience.
